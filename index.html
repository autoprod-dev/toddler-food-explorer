About

Offering a food many times — even when it's only touched, sniffed or licked — is how many toddlers slowly come to accept it. Keep it low-pressure: a small amount next to a food they already like, and no pressure to eat.

This is a tracker, not medical advice. Talk to your GP, child health nurse or a paediatric dietitian about feeding concerns.

Some foods need extra care for children under 3: whole nuts, whole grapes, popcorn and hard raw vegetables or fruit. Quarter grapes and cherry tomatoes, grate or steam carrot and apple, spread nut butters thinly, and always supervise.
